# Security

Report a security issue in this repository by email to security@lightdrawer.org.

## In scope

This MCP server, and the way it handles local files and LightDrawer receipts.

## Out of scope

Third-party MCP clients are out of scope. The public witnesses themselves are out of scope. That includes OpenTimestamps calendars, FreeTSA, Certificate Transparency logs, and Rekor.

## What to expect

I will try to respond. There is no bounty.
